Background art in assets/bg/
============================

All artwork in this folder was created by Luis Zuno (@ansimuz) - https://ansimuz.com
and released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Attribution is not required, but credit is given here with thanks.

The files are 1:1 pixel copies of the original layers. They were re-cut by
scripts/bg_assets.py (split/padded to a common height, moon and tree separated
into sprites, losslessly re-encoded). No pixels were scaled or recoloured,
except the small patch of night sky behind the moon, which was repainted with
the sky's own two colours.
